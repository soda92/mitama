## Name map — two sisters, two aliases

This is the easiest place to get confused:

```text
Older sister:
  真木碧 / Maki Midori
  adult alias: 伊藤瞳子 / Itou Touko
  Mitama role: Ran

Younger sister:
  伊藤麻衣 / Itou Mai
  adult alias: 伊藤硝子 / Itou Shouko
  Mitama role: none
```

**Touko** and **Shouko** are different people. Touko is Maki Midori's adult
alias; Shouko is Itou Mai's.

## Career

Itou Mai debuted in 1999 as a first-generation member of the voice unit
**KiraKira☆Melody Gakuen**, the same unit her older sister was in, and left the
unit that same year.

She was later affiliated with **Atelier Peach**, **Media Force** until March
2009, and **Jac in Production** until around 2020. She is currently listed with
**Eining**.

Like her sister, most of her game work is in adult games under the **Itou
Shouko** alias. Her general-audience work is mostly supporting roles in anime,
film, OVA, drama CDs, narration and voice-over.
