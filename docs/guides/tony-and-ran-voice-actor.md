# Tony and Ran's voice actor

> Creator and cast reference for **御魂 ～忍～ / Mitama ~Shinobi~**.
> This is a credits/works note, not an adult-content guide.

## Tony (Tony Taka)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Professional name | **Tony** / Tony Taka (トニー) |
| Real name | 田中 貴之 (Tanaka Takayuki) |
| Born | 1971, Miyagi Prefecture |
| Roles | Illustrator, character designer, video-game artist |
| Company | Executive director of RPM |
| *Mitama* credit | 原画 (character art) |

Tony studied art and design, worked in advertising, and moved into
manga/anime-style illustration around 1998. He quickly became known for adult
game character design before expanding into general games, light novels, art
books and figure-related work.

### Tony's Ciel period

*Mitama* belongs to the early **Tony + Ciel** period. Selected Ciel/Tony works:

| 2003 | *After...* | Tony art; さとだみつひこ scenario |

The shared scenario writer across *Arcana*, *Mitama* and *After...* is
**さとだみつひこ**, so *Mitama* sits inside a continuing Ciel/Tony creative
line rather than being an isolated collaboration.

### Wider work

Tony later became widely known for Sega's **Shining** series and other general
game work, including:

- *Shining Tears* (2004)
- *Shining Wind* (2007)
- *Shining Hearts* (2010)
- *Shining Blade* (2012)
- *Shining Ark* (2013)
- *Shining Resonance* (2014)
- *Exstetra* (2013)
- character design work for *Azur Lane*
- *Tony's Art Works Graph I–IV* and other art books

### What *Mitama* did — and did not — do for Tony

The observable continuities are:

- it was an early entry in Tony's adult-game portfolio;
- it strengthened the **Tony + Ciel** brand;
- Ciel continued commissioning Tony for *After...*, *Sora no Iro* and
  *Shin Shou Genmukan*;
- the historical/fantasy costume work sits comfortably beside Tony's later
  fantasy character design.

But it is too strong to say *Mitama* caused the later *Shining* career. A safer
summary is:

> *Mitama* helped establish Tony's early adult-game reputation and his
> association with Ciel. It was part of the portfolio that made his later work
> possible, not the origin of it.

There was no direct *Mitama* sequel, and its battle/ADV hybrid did not become
Ciel's house style. Its legacy is mainly Tony's early art, Ciel's Tony-era
brand, the ninja/kunoichi premise and a cult reputation.

For the market context around this period, see
[cultural and market context](mitama-culture-context.md).

## Ran's voice actor

Ran (蘭) is the strong-willed kunoichi in 八尋's Southern Court route. She is
the daughter of a ninja family, treats 八尋 like a child, and is bright and
talkative while still capable of cold adherence to ninja rules.

### One person, several names

Ran's voice credit is **伊藤 瞳子 / Itou Touko**, which is an adult-game alias
of **真木 碧 / Maki Midori**.

| Name | Reading | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **真木 碧** | Maki Midori | Main/general and console credit name |
| **伊藤 瞳子** | Itou Touko | Adult-game alias; Ran |
| **相葉 茉美** | Aiba Mami | Another adult-game alias |
| **叶芽 枢** | Kaname Kururu | Another adult-game alias |

Her real name is **伊藤 麻喜 (Itou Maki)**. Her younger sister, **伊藤 麻衣
(Itou Mai)**, is also a voice actress. The aliases are the reason searches can
turn up what look like several different people.

### Game voice and OVA voice

The original game data is not self-contained for voice. This project mounts the
separate first-press **voice disc** as the `disc` pack: 8,053 `.XA` files plus
`kunoichi.asc`. The DVD asset set itself is mainly BGM and SE.

The 2009 Milky / MS Pictures OVA adaptation of *Mitama* has two episodes
(directed by Takeshi Yoda). Anime News Network credits Ran as **Midori Maki
(as Touko Itou)** — the same person under both names.

So the game and the OVA are different productions, but the Ran casting belongs
to the same voice actress.

伊藤瞳子 / Itou Touko
相葉茉美 / Aiba Mami
叶芽枢 / Kaname Kururu

material. Check tags before starting a title. The sources below include her
VNDB and Wikipedia pages, which are the best starting points.
